I tested 9 properties and reported 1 bug.

The bug is an inconsistency in how delimiters are split. A space followed by a combining mark (`" \u{301}"`) is split inside that character when `Space` is the only boundary. The result is `"_\u{301}"`. If you add a boundary that can't match, such as `LowerUpper`, there is no split and the result is `" \u{301}"`. The cause is the byte-level fast path in `boundary::split`, which ignores grapheme clusters. The repro is `repro_fast_vs_general_path.rs`, and it fails as expected. The finding is in `findings.json`.

The other 8 properties passed, including the overwrite, remove-boundaries and `Converter`-agreement checks.
