I tested 7 properties and reported 1 bug: `relative_time` drops digits when `ndigits` is large.

- **Reported:** `relative_time` formats the number with `:g`, which keeps only 6 significant digits. With `ndigits=6` and a 1.123456 s difference it returns `'1.12346 seconds ago'`. `decimal_relative_time` correctly returns 1.123456. The repro fails for that reason.
- **Rejected as not defensible:**
  - `isoparse` fails on timezone-aware ISO strings, but its docstring only promises a limited subset.
  - `parse_timedelta('')` returns a zero `timedelta` instead of raising, and the docstring doesn't clearly require a raise.
  - `strpdate` failed on year 999, which comes from `strftime` not zero-padding `%Y` on this platform, not from the project.
