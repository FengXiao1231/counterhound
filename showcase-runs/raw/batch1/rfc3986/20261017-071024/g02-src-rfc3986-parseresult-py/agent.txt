I tested 11 properties and reported 1 bug: `ParseResultBytes.unsplit()` raises `AttributeError` on any URI without an authority, such as `b"/a"`. The repro in `test_repro_bytes_unsplit_no_authority.py` fails now for that reason.

The same crash hits `ParseResultBytes.authority` on those inputs, which is where the error is raised (`parseresult.py:409`). `ParseResult.from_string("/a").unsplit()` works.

I left two other failures out as not defensible:
- **`split_authority("[")`:** it raises `ValueError`, and so does non-strict `ParseResult.from_string("//[")`. The standard library's `urlparse` also raises `ValueError` there.
- **`split_authority("1@")`:** it returns host `None` where the strict parser gives `""`, a trivial difference between the lenient and strict paths.
